# Hello, World.
Apparently, after spending a decade designing things for other people's websites, I've finally decided to build my own little computer. This is it. Welcome to **Perdana's Computer**. It's a personal space for me to document what I'm learning, building, breaking, and figuring out along the way — somewhere between visual design, product design, technology, and code.

And there's something slightly funny about starting this now. More than a decade ago, I was already playing around with websites. I had an old computer, a Blogspot site, and an unhealthy amount of curiosity. I learned bits of HTML, experimented with CSS, and tried to figure out how things on the internet worked. Then I somehow ended up spending the next ten years as a visual designer.

Now I'm slowly finding my way back to the computer.

This time, I want to do more than design what something looks like. I want to understand how it works. I want to learn how to design it, build it, and eventually ship it.

## Why I'm writing
I'm currently exploring the intersection of **visual design, product design, UX, and design engineering**. I'm learning code again. I'm experimenting with design in code. I'm building things instead of only designing them.

I don't have everything figured out yet. That's actually why I'm writing.

This blog will be where I document the process — the projects, experiments, things that work, things that don't, and the things I learn along the way. Some of it will probably be polished. Some of it probably won't. That's okay.

## What's coming next
The next article is a much longer story. It's about how I went from an old computer in a small city, to making websites as a kid, to discovering design, starting with a $5 freelance job, working across 3,000+ projects, building a small design business, and eventually finding myself here — exploring **Product Design and Design Engineering**.

It's a story about where I came from, why I'm changing direction, and why this feels less like starting over and more like coming back to something I left behind.

But for now:

**Hello, World.**

The computer is finally online.

— Perdana
